## A. WINNING MICRO-NICHE

**Large-print, easy bold-line coloring paperback for seniors with low vision**  
*(a vision-friendly adult coloring micro-niche)*

A paperback coloring book designed specifically for adults 65+ with age-related vision decline (and secondary buyers: adult children / caregivers / senior centers). Differentiation is built into the interior: genuinely large bold line art, one design per page, single-sided thick white paper to prevent marker bleed-through, high-contrast black-on-white, large-print title/instructions, and a lay-flat-friendly binding — all features that existing "large print" senior coloring books inconsistently deliver.

## C. TARGET AUDIENCE

**Primary customer:** Adults 65+ experiencing age-related vision decline who still want to color but find standard adult coloring books too detailed, small-print, or frustrating. Includes seniors with low vision, early-stage dementia/Alzheimer's, and limited hand dexterity.

**Secondary customers:**
- Adult children and caregivers buying accessible, stress-relief gifts for parents/grandparents.
- Senior centers, activity coordinators, and memory-care facilities buying in bulk for group activities.

**Purchasing motivations:**
- Accessibility: genuinely large, bold, high-contrast designs that don't strain aging eyes.
- Frustration-free experience: one design per page, no cramped grids, no "large print" claims that under-deliver.
- Practical usability: thick single-sided paper so markers don't bleed through; lay-flat/easy-to-hold format.
- Gifting: evergreen, non-seasonal gift for birthdays, holidays, Mother's/Father's Day.
- Wellness: stress relief, cognitive stimulation, fine-motor maintenance, screen-free leisure.

## G. MARKET GAP

**What existing books are missing:**
1. **Verified accessibility specs** — published paper weight, confirmed single-sided thick paper, guaranteed no-bleed for markers. "Large print" is currently an unregulated marketing claim on KDP (kdpeasy, 2026).
2. **Lay-flat / spiral binding** — repeatedly requested by senior buyers; most competitors are perfect-bound paperbacks that don't lay flat and strain hands near the gutter.
3. **Genuinely bold, simple, one-design-per-page layouts** — many "easy/senior" books still include small intricate spaces that frustrate low-vision users.
4. **High-contrast black-on-white + large-print title/instructions** — not consistently delivered.
5. **A title that explicitly signals the vision-friendly promise** so caregivers and seniors can find it via search (the theme/keyword is the positioning).

**The opportunity:** A vision-friendly senior coloring paperback that publishes its accessibility specs (paper weight, single-sided, large print, bold lines), uses lay-flat-friendly binding, and markets directly to low-vision seniors and their gift-buying adult children. This sits inside the high-competition, proven-demand adult-coloring parent market but serves an underserved sub-audience.

## H. PRODUCT CONCEPT

| Element | Recommendation |
|---------|----------------|
| **Format** | Paperback, 8.5 × 11 in (large coloring format), single-sided pages |
| **Book type** | Large-print easy bold-line coloring book (medium-content activity book) |
| **Core content & features** | 50–60 large, bold, simple designs (nature, flowers, everyday objects — familiar, calming themes); one design per page; single-sided 60+ lb white paper to prevent bleed-through; high-contrast black line art; large-print title, subtitle, and usage instructions; optional perforated/removable pages; lay-flat-friendly binding; brief "designed for low vision" note on cover |
| **Page-count range** | 100–120 pages (50–60 designs single-sided + title/blank/solution pages) |
| **Suggested price** | $8.99–$9.99 (premium but accessible; logbooks/specialty journals tolerate $9.99–$14.99; coloring books typically $6.99–$9.99) |
| **Differentiation strategy** | Publish accessibility specs competitors hide; bold+large+single-sided+lay-flat as a bundle; explicit low-vision/senior positioning in title & keywords; one design per page; no cramped "large print" designs |
| **Why customers prefer it** | It actually delivers on the "large print / easy / bold" promise for aging eyes, doesn't bleed through with markers, is physically easier to hold and lay flat, and is findable by caregivers searching for vision-friendly senior activities. |

## I. COMMERCIAL POTENTIAL

KDP paperback royalty = 60% of list price − printing cost. Printing (US, B&W) = $0.85 fixed + $0.012/page (KDP table, `backend/kdp_engine/profit.py`, `backend/kdp_engine/config.py`). These are **ACTUAL KDP formula calculations** (not sales projections — no income is promised).

| 80 | $8.99 | $1.81 | **$3.58** | ~40% | $3.02 |
| 100 | $8.99 | $2.05 | **$3.34** | ~37% | $3.42 |
| 100 | $9.99 | $2.05 | **$3.94** | ~39% | $3.42 |
| 80 | $7.99 | $1.81 | **$2.98** | ~37% | $3.02 |

**Interpretation:** At 80 pages / $8.99, the paperback earns ≈ $3.58 per sale — a solid royalty for a low-content paperback. Pricing above $9.99 risks conversion unless differentiation is strong; below $6.99 reads as low value and suppresses margin (KDPEasy guidance). No sales or monthly-income figures are promised; actual sell-through depends on listing quality, ads, reviews, and ranking.
