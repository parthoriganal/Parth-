# KDP Coloring Book Prompt Kit — Large-Print Bold-Line for Seniors (Low Vision)

**Winning niche:** Large-print easy bold-line coloring paperback for seniors with low vision  
**Format:** 8.5" x 11" paperback, single-sided pages, bold black outlines on white background  
**Target page count:** 40–60 pages (InkChamps sweet spot: avg 118 reviews at $8.99, 40–60 page sweet spot)  
**Cover:** Matte, senior-friendly, high contrast, large readable title

---

## Part 1 — Master Prompt Template (Copy/Paste)

Use this template for every interior page. Replace `[SUBJECT]` and `[THEME-DETAILS]`.

```
A senior-friendly coloring book page, 8.5x11 inch aspect ratio, single subject in the center, 
[SUBJECT], [THEME-DETAILS], bold thick black outlines, large open spaces, easy to color, 
no fine details, no small intricate patterns, no shading, no gradients, no gray tones, 
pure white background, high contrast black line art, minimalist composition, plenty of white space 
around the subject, one large subject only, suitable for seniors with low vision, 
vector style, clean crisp lines, professional coloring book interior page, no text, no watermark, 
no signature, no logo, no border frame around edges --ar 3:4 --stylize 100 --v 6
```

**Key parameters to keep consistent across all pages:**
- `bold thick black outlines` — critical for low vision
- `large open spaces` — easy to stay inside lines
- `pure white background` — clean KDP interior
- `no fine details / no small intricate patterns` — avoids frustration
- `one large subject only` — uncluttered composition
- `--stylize 100` (Midjourney) — keeps it simple, not artistic/abstract

## Part 2 — Negative Prompt (what to AVOID)

```
fine details, intricate patterns, small elements, shading, gradients, gray, 
grayscale, color, colored, text, words, letters, watermark, signature, artist name, 
logo, border, frame, cluttered, busy, complex, realistic photography, 3d render, 
blurry, fuzzy, thin lines, multiple subjects, crowded composition
```

## Part 6 — Batch Generation Workflow

1. **Generate cover first** (1 image, iterate until title is readable and large)
2. **Generate interior pages in batches** (5–10 prompts per session, save each as PNG)
3. **Upscale** all images to 300 DPI (2550 x 3300 pixels for 8.5x11")
4. **Convert to grayscale** if any color leaked in
5. **Assemble in Canva/Word/InDesign** with page numbers and blank back sides
6. **Export as PDF** with crop marks for KDP
7. **Order proof copy** before publishing
