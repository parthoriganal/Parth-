## A Note on the Evidence

This book distinguishes, throughout, between what is established, what is emerging, and what is hypothesis. Where the research is divided, it says so rather than choosing the convenient side.

The evidence cited is drawn from systematic reviews, meta-analyses, clinical practice guidelines, and primary studies, listed in the References at the end. Certainty language follows the conventions of the field: when a review rates evidence as "moderate certainty," that is a specific technical judgement and it is not the same as "proven."

Where a study was read only in abstract or summary form, the reference list does not claim otherwise. No references have been fabricated and no studies are cited that were not consulted.
