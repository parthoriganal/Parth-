# MAKE FULL BOOK — Copy-Paste Prompt Kit
**Winning niche:** Large-print easy bold-line coloring paperback for seniors with low vision  
**Format:** 8.5" x 11" KDP paperback, single-sided, black & white interior  
**Page target:** 50 pages (25 illustrations + 25 blank backsides)  
**Price target:** $8.99–$9.99 (InkChamps sweet spot, 60% royalty tier)

---

## HOW TO USE THIS KIT

1. Pick an image generator: **Midjourney v6**, **DALL-E 3**, **Stable Diffusion XL**, or **Ideogram**
2. Copy each prompt below, paste it in, generate one image per page
3. Save every image as PNG at 300 DPI (2550 × 3300 px for 8.5" × 11")
4. Assemble into a PDF (Canva, Word, or InDesign) — illustration on front, blank page on back
5. Export print-ready PDF, upload to KDP, order proof, publish

---

## PART 1 — MASTER PROMPT TEMPLATE (use for every interior page)

**Midjourney / Stable Diffusion version:**

```
A senior-friendly coloring book page, one large subject centered, [SUBJECT], [DETAILS], 
bold thick black outlines, large open spaces, easy to color, no fine details, no small 
intricate patterns, no shading, no gradients, no gray tones, pure white background, high 
contrast black line art, minimalist composition, plenty of white space around the subject, 
one subject only, suitable for seniors with low vision, clean crisp vector line art, no text, 
no watermark, no signature, no logo, no border frame --ar 3:4 --stylize 100 --v 6
```

**DALL-E 3 version (no -- parameters, write it as text):**

```
A senior-friendly coloring book page in vertical portrait orientation. One large subject 
centered on the page: [SUBJECT], [DETAILS]. Bold thick black outlines. Large open spaces 
easy to color inside. No fine details, no small intricate patterns, no shading, no gradients, 
no gray tones. Pure white background. High contrast black line art on white. Minimalist 
composition with plenty of white space around the subject. One subject only, uncluttered. 
Suitable for seniors with low vision. Clean crisp vector-style line art. No text, no 
watermark, no signature, no logo, no border frame around the edges.
```

**Negative prompt (Stable Diffusion / Ideogram):**

```
fine details, intricate patterns, small elements, shading, gradients, gray, grayscale, 
color, colored, text, words, letters, watermark, signature, artist name, logo, border, 
frame, cluttered, busy, complex, realistic photography, 3d render, blurry, fuzzy, thin 
lines, multiple subjects, crowded composition
```

**Consistency rules — never change these across pages:**
- Bold thick black outlines
- Large open spaces
- Pure white background
- No fine details / no shading / no gray
- One subject only
- No text, watermark, or signature

## PART 6 — ASSEMBLY WORKFLOW (Canva / Word / InDesign)

1. **Create new design:** 8.5" × 11" (or 2550 × 3300 px at 300 DPI)
2. **Upload cover:** Front cover as page 1
3. **Add blank pages:** Page 2 blank (inside front cover)
4. **Add title page:** Page 3 with title in large font
5. **Add copyright page:** Page 4 with your name, year, ISBN placeholder
6. **Add blank page:** Page 5
7. **Add illustrations:** Page 6 = illustration 1 (right side), Page 7 = blank (left side)
8. **Repeat:** illustrations 2–25 on right-side pages only, blank on left-side backs
9. **Add back cover:** Last page = back cover design
10. **Export:** PDF Print, 300 DPI, crop marks if available
11. **Order proof:** Order one physical copy from KDP before publishing
12. **Publish:** Set price $8.99–$9.99, select US marketplace, choose Black & White interior
