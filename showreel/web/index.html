The idea

A single continuous piece of motion design built from eight chapters that each change the rules of the image — depth, material, typography, damage, and light. Every frame is generated from pure 2D vector, particle and typographic systems: no 3D models, no stock footage, no templates. Depth is faked with projection maths, parallax and layered light.
