02 — FIELD

The object
unfolds
as you scroll.

i

Chrome shell

A single icosahedron, metalness 1, roughness 0.06 — it reflects the orbit instead of being lit by it.

ii

Orbital cage

Three rings on independent axes. Each is a real torus in the same world, not a drawn overlay.

iii

Dispersion

Scroll past halfway and the shell expands into shards. The colour split is chromatic, taken from the library's two families.

iv

Reassembly

Every part returns. Nothing is restarted, nothing is reloaded — the same meshes just move.

    ENERGY 0.00

    What you're touching

    A 6 000-point spring lattice on a single canvas. Your pointer injects velocity; every point relaxes back with damping. The marks you leave decay over about four seconds.

    Why it's here

    The library's colours drive the particle temperature — energy raises hue from Iris toward Fuschia. Same six values, a different kind of light.
